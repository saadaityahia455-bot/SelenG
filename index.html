My Letter Back to You

Selen,

I hope this letter finds you well —
with your pink hair still glowing,
your heart still full of songs,
and your quiet village dreams still alive.

Receiving your sweet box was the happiest moment of my summer.
Before looking at the gifts, I looked at your note.
Your handwriting, slightly tilted and delicate,
showed all the effort you put in.

Sending this box of memories all the way from Morocco,
across borders, flights, and miles,
every item was wrapped so carefully,
as if protecting a precious treasure.

From the playful golden scroll to the cute little charms,
I felt every bit of your warmth
My heart is full knowing how much I mean to you.

You told me about your surgery bars, your mother's matching outfits,
your brother's betrayal, and the evil eye superstition.
You told me about Adéla, KiiiKiii, and "Ain't in LA."
You told me about the Morocco trip that almost was —
and the quiet Italian village that still might be.
I remember all of it.

I might not have grand words,
but I made this webpage to unfold your sweet gestures.
The quirky doll sits on my bag,
the bone charm keeps me company,

You told me you were born "as wild as a flood."
And I've learned that floods don't destroy —
they reshape, they nourish, they bring life.
You've reshaped my heart in ways I never expected.

Yours always,
Saad
